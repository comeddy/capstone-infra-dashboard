🦿 GapONet closes humanoid sim-to-real gap by payload
New operator predicts residual actions based on what a robot carries, trained on 11,298+ real-sim motion pairs (TWINS). Cuts large sim-to-real errors to under 0.09% vs ~10% for rivals. (Nov 13)

📡 X Square Robot open-sources XINFER, a fleet VLA server
Hundreds of its VLA robots already run in production (parcel handling). XINFER's scheduling hits 1.26-3.75x more concurrent robots per GPU than prior serving systems. (Nov 11)

🌡️ Thermo-VLA gives a Unitree G1 a sense of temperature
Residual encoder injects hot/cold cues into RGB tokens for picking cold bottles or spotting overheated targets: 0.86 avg success, +18pts over a baseline, best unseen-object generalization. (Nov 9)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.11981
