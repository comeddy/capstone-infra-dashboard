🦿 GapONet closes the humanoid sim-to-real gap, payload by payload
Learns residual actions from what a robot carries, trained on 11,298+ real-sim motion pairs. Cuts large sim-to-real errors to under 0.09% vs ~10% for rivals. (Nov 13)

📡 X Square Robot open-sources XINFER, a fleet-scale VLA server

🌡️ Thermo-VLA gives a Unitree G1 a sense of temperature
A residual encoder injects hot/cold cues into RGB tokens for picking cold bottles or spotting overheated targets: 0.86 avg success, +18pts over a π0.5 baseline, best unseen-object generalization. (Nov 9)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.11981
