🏠 Apple's HALO: a humanoid trained only in simulated homes, 91% real-world success. 1,000 procedural homes, zero teleop data. Zero-shot: 91% over 360 trials in 6 unmapped rooms — sits in chairs, steps through doors, crawls under tables. (Nov 4)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.06908

📦 PKU, NUS, MIT & NVIDIA open-source a 50,000-hour egocentric data recipe. Open-HumanWAM pretrains on human video alone; after robot post-training it sets new SOTA on RoboTwin 2.0 (92.7%/84.6%). Full dataset+code+weights released. (Nov 4-5)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.06817

📚 New survey maps dexterous robot hands across hardware, data, learning & eval. Xi'an Jiaotong U. reviews Jan 2024–Nov 2026 progress — hand cost now $1K-$6.5K — and flags 11 open problems, incl. cross-sensor tactile generalization.
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.06724
