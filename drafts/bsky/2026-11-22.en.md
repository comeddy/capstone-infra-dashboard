🧠 Reasoning + acting ≠ sum of parts
Tsinghua, Oxford, Stanford, CMU, Baidu Cloud & more built Mind2Act World: 30 tasks testing embodied reasoning & execution together. Top agent: 52.7% reasoning-only, 39.0% action-only, just 12.0% combined. (Nov 19)

🤲 A state machine rescues a stuck VLA
Oxford's PGX wraps frozen π0.5/GR00T VLAs in a predicate-gated state machine + world-frame locking, no retraining. Real Unitree G1 coffee-fetching success: π0.5 0%→60%, GR00T 35%→80%. (Nov 19)
https://kl-research.github.io/pgx

🚗🦾 One backbone, driving + manipulation

🔍 Grading GPT-6 Astra's robot moves
