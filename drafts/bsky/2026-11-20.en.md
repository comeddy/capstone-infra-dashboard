🥽 A humanoid trained on smart-glasses footage alone
Notre Dame's E2S2H turns Aria-glasses recordings into sim-verified robot data, no teleop needed. A GR00T N1.7 policy trained on it hit 45% bottle-lift & 25% hat-wearing on a real Unitree G1. (Nov 14)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.19196

🛠️ Coding agents that fix a VLA's own mistakes
Tsinghua/Striding AI/AGIBot's Agentic-Teacher diagnoses failed VLA rollouts, writes & tests repair code, then retrains on the fixes. π0 success: 79→88% LIBERO-10, 80→91% MetaWorld-10, 0→30% RoboCasa. (Nov 12)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.17394

🔁 Keeping long tasks on track, no retraining needed
Purdue/DEVCOM ARL's RePlan-VLA monitors progress and replans around failed subtasks, for any frozen VLA. +21.9% success on RoboCasa365, +10.4% on CALVIN vs best; tested on a real Franka arm. (Nov 18)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.23843
