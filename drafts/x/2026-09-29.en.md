🌀 D-Robotics open-sources Uranus, a robot world-model simulator
A diffusion sim that streams RGB video frame-by-frame from joint trajectories in real time (24 FPS), across single-arm, dual-arm & humanoid embodiments. Code, weights & SDK released.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24815
