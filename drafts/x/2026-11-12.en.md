🤖 Physical AI Daily (Nov 12)
NVIDIA's GRAIL is a CoRL Best Paper finalist (84-90% real G1 success), Tokyo's VLA Handover cuts cloud calls 49-77%, Swiss PRoMiSe moves multi-robot patrol to real Go2s, and a CoRL study finds world-model nav still loses to A* in the field.
🧵

---

🧠 NVIDIA's GRAIL: humanoid robot data generated fully digitally — 3D assets + video priors, no physical collection. Trained only on this data, a Unitree G1 hit 84% pick-up / 90% stair-climb success. CoRL 2026 Best Paper finalist.
https://research.nvidia.com/labs/dair/grail/

---

📡 VLA Handover (Tokyo): robot runs a small on-device VLA, calls a big server VLA only when failure looks likely. LIBERO: +11-22pt success over the small model alone, 49-77% fewer server requests than always-on cloud control.
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.08987

---

🐕 PRoMiSe (Laelaps AI/ETH Zurich): multi-robot patrol policy trained in 2D sim, no graph or planner, deployed zero-shot on real Unitree Go2s. Indoor coverage within 1pt of sim; worked at a site 8x training-map size. CoRL 2026.
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.promise-multi-robot-monitoring

---

🌍 CoRL finding: a world-model nav planner beat A* on trajectory error in a controlled lab twin, but in real field deployment success fell to 55% vs A*'s 97%. (DEVCOM ARL/NYU/Berkeley)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.world-models-robot-navigation
