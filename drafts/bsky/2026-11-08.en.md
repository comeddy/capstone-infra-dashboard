🏠 Apple's HALO: trained entirely in simulated homes, hits 91% in the real world
1,000 procedurally generated homes, zero teleop data. Zero-shot: 91% success over 360 trials in 6 unmapped rooms — sits in chairs, steps through doors, crawls under tables. (Nov 4)
