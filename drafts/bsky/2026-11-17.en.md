🕹️ Teaching an open model to "use" real robots like a computer
MIT/Berkeley/UCSD/NVIDIA's PEAR trains a 27B reasoning model via RL purely in simulation, no demos needed. LIBERO success jumps 1.6%→74.4%; a real dual-arm robot goes 0%→60%. Code open. (Nov 16)
https://github.com/mit-han-lab/pear

---

🧭 A humanoid walks itself from an office lobby to the street
AgiBot's X-Nav takes a Lingxi X2 humanoid ~100m indoors-to-outdoors on one instruction: through a door, past obstacles, to a store next door. Its MELIOR models run on RGB only. (Nov 12)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.17598

---

🎥 Letting robots learn from human videos never meant for them
SALA (Sharpa Robotics, SJTU, Tsinghua) bridges unpaired human/robot clips via language into one latent-action space. More human-video pretraining lowers robot error, lifts success. (Nov 12)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.17110

---

🗂️ A new open dataset chases a "scaling law" for physical AI
Ropedia Xperience 2: 1,589 3D-scanned scenes + 4,230 egocentric recordings (131h, 74 tasks), reused for sim environments, motion retargeting and benchmarks. Code & data public. (Nov 12)
https://github.com/Ropedia/Ropedia-Xperience-2
