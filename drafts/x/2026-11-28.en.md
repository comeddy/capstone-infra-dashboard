🤖 Physical AI Daily (Nov 28)
FineDex brings finger-level control to a real humanoid, WorldReforge teaches world models to handle failed grasps, TU Munich finds world models can't feel force, and DemoFoundry closes the sim-to-real data gap.

---

🖐️ Finger-level control, real humanoid
Berkeley/Harvard's FineDex lets you pick *which* finger a robot uses. On a real Unitree G1 + Wuji hands, finger-switch success hits 83.9% vs ≤8.0% for prior methods. (Nov 25)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.34884

---

🧠 Teaching world models what "wrong" looks like
Zhejiang Univ's WorldReforge trains on 62K simulated slips & failed grasps, not just successes. Cuts object-motion error 28.2% (35.0% on unseen objects). (Nov 25)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.33925

---

🫳 Can a world model feel force?
TU Munich probes video/world/policy models for "force awareness." Most sense *when* contact happens, not *how much* force — post-training doesn't fix it. (Nov 25)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.34422

---

🎬 Coding agents write robot training data
CUHK-Shenzhen/DexForce's DemoFoundry pairs coding agents + generative rendering for photoreal synthetic demos. Trained policies near real-demo level (21/30 vs 5/30, new lighting). (Nov 25)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.34671
