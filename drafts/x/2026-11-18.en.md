🧠 Scaling a VLA model to 552B parameters
Tsinghua/PKU/SJTU/Galbot's ScaleVLA pairs a 552B backbone with a trainable action head. 10 demos beat a 2B backbone's 50; LIBERO-PRO 45.1% vs 31.3%; charger insertion 4/10→9/10, 216ms latency. (Nov 17)
https://galaxygeneralrobotics.github.io/scalevla/

---

⚡ Training a humanoid in under a minute
TU Darmstadt's XMimic & XSoma train a Unitree G1 via a world model split across 4 H100s. XMimic: all 40 LAFAN1 clips in 0.86min, 3.7x faster. XSoma: 12.3x faster than FastSAC on rough terrain. Code open. (Nov 16)
https://github.com/nico-bohlinger/xmimic

---

🖐️ Metric-scale hand motion capture, in the wild
CityU HK & CyberOrigin's WinDEX reconstructs real-world-scale 3D hand poses from stereo video for teleop & RL, via 1,000 hrs of data. RL policies trained on it hit 7.6x prior methods' success. (Nov 16)
https://fruitrabbit.github.io/WinDEX/

---

😊 A 34-actuator robot face learns to express itself
Bristol Robotics Lab's ROSE packs 34 actuators into a 19.5cm head, driven by a shared human-robot facial-motion space from text or audio. Runs at 60fps, beating baselines in user ratings. (Nov 16)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.20950
