💰 $47M to collect robot training data
Bengaluru's Humyn Labs raised a $47M Series A (Accel-led), recording human work as training data for humanoid, world-model & voice-AI builders. 12 paying customers, 20+ countries. (Nov 18)

🧠 50,000 hrs of human video teaches a humanoid chores
EngineAI's Awaken-HI pretrains on egocentric human motion, fine-tunes on its T800 robot: 93.75% mean success on 8 tasks, beating GR00T N1.7 & π0.5 by ~35pts. (Nov 19)
https://engineai-robotics.github.io/Awaken-HI

🔁 Closing the sim-to-real loop with a world model
Amazon/Stanford/UT Austin's WEVO post-trains a whole-body VLA inside a video world model, skipping risky real robot RL. Success: 23.3%→75.0% on a Unitree G1, beats real-robot RL's 48.3%. (Nov 18)
https://qianzhong-chen.github.io/wevo.github.io/

🏃 An open benchmark for humanoid whole-body control
TU Darmstadt & DFKI's LocoMuJoCo covers 9 humanoids + quadrupeds, ships mocap datasets & sim-to-real tooling for the Unitree G1 & AgiBot X2 Ultra. MIT licensed. (Nov 19)
https://github.com/robfiras/loco-mujoco
