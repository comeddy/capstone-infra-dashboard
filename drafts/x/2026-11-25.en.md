🤖 Physical AI Daily (Nov 25)
A 10,807-env benchmark exposes a huge open-source robot-agent gap, Michigan ships a $6,800 open-source humanoid, Berkeley/NVIDIA build self-improving simulators, and a study maps what egocentric video teaches robots.

🧠 Agentic robots get their SWE-bench moment
Tsinghua/Einsia.AI et al. built RoboUniverse-10K: 10,807 real-to-sim envs for coding-agent robot control. Frontier models hit 85.8% success; open models lag at 2.6-10.0%. Fine-tuned model: 0.8%→21.2%. (Nov 23)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.31002

🦾 A humanoid you can afford to build
Michigan's Arcadian: open-source 1.11m/20kg humanoid, no CNC, no custom PCBs or actuators, ~$6,800 total. Metal-embedded 3D-printed shells are 8.3x stiffer than plastic. RL locomotion & VR teleop work. (Nov 23)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.29868

🌍 Simulators that build and fix themselves
Berkeley, NVIDIA, UIUC & CMU's Real2Worlds2Real turns a few real photos into digital twins plus diverse "digital cousins," then runs its own data gen, RL & imitation learning, reusing skills as it goes. (Nov 23)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.31098

📹 What human video teaches a robot
Tsinghua, Shanghai Qi Zhi & HKUST's EgoStudy scales egocentric video for VLA vs. world-action-model training. Helps wrist-motion prediction more than finger skill — WAMs win on scarce data, VLAs at scale. (Nov 23)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.30490
