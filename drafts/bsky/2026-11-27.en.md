🧠 One action space for 15 kinds of robots
Horizon Robotics' HoloBrain-0.5 VLA standardizes joint & camera conventions across embodiments. Transfers zero-shot to new arms (63.3%) & lifts a Unitree G1 humanoid to 70.2% success, vs 11.0% for π0.5. (Nov 25)

🛡️ Teaching a robot to imagine risk first
NVIDIA/NTU/KTH's WAtcher previews an action's outcome before executing, retreating if risky. Hazardous trials drop 27.0%→10.8%, no success loss. Its Safe-Guard module beats GPT-6-Astra at spotting hazards. (Nov 25)
