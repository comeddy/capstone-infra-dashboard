🧠 Halving VLA control "stalls"
Peking University's SR-VLA predicts next-step inputs mid-execution, repairs its KV cache only when reality differs. 2.67x faster queries on π0.5, success holds 96.9% vs 96.4% sync. (Nov 17)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.22356

---

🎬 Teaching robots to spot their own failures
NVIDIA's FailureGen grafts real failure motions onto new scenes via a Cosmos world model, synthesizing failure video. Detectors trained on it hit 0.51 avg precision vs 0.41 real-only. (Nov 17)
https://akul-nv.github.io/failuregen/

🚪 A humanoid that turns doorknobs by feel
Amazon/Rice/Michigan's TMA reads tactile force direction & magnitude to adapt a Unitree G1's grip online, trained sim-only. Zero-shot on 4 real doorknobs, crushing baselines that often can't even hold on. (Nov 13)
https://humanoid-tma.github.io

🤝 Humanoid teams that sync without network comms
USC/VinMotion's HEAVE-HO trains on just 2 G1s to carry tables, hand off boxes, and squeeze through doors together, then scales to 10-robot & human-mixed teams, no retraining — just speech cues. (Nov 17)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.22275

📊 What 200K+ hours of robot teleop data taught AgiBot
Parallelizing operator control cuts task time 43-53%; compliant force control hits 100% success vs 80-87% for rigid position control; 1 miscalibrated robot failed all 30 trials. (Nov 17)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.22388
