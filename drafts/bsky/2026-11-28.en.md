🤖 Physical AI Daily (Nov 28)
Berkeley/Harvard's FineDex gives a real humanoid finger-level control, Zhejiang University teaches world models what failed grasps look like, TU Munich finds world models can't feel force, and a coding-agent pipeline closes the sim-to-real data gap.

---

🖐️ Finger-level control on a real humanoid
Berkeley/Harvard's FineDex lets you specify *which* finger a robot uses, not just task success. On a real Unitree G1 + Wuji hands, finger-switch success hits 83.9% vs ≤8.0% for prior HOI methods. (Nov 25)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.34884

---

🧠 Teaching world models what "wrong" looks like
Zhejiang Univ/Shanghai Innovation Institute's WorldReforge trains on 62K simulated slips & failed grasps, not just successes. Cuts object-motion error 28.2% (35.0% on unseen objects). (Nov 25)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.33925

---

🫳 Can a world model feel how hard it's gripping?
TU Munich probes frozen video/world/policy models for "force awareness." Most detect *when* contact happens but not *how much* force is applied — and robot-specific post-training doesn't close the gap. (Nov 25)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.34422

---

🎬 Coding agents write the robot's training data
CUHK-Shenzhen/DexForce's DemoFoundry pairs coding agents w/ generative rendering for photoreal synthetic demos, no human demos needed. Trained policies near real-demo level (21/30 vs 5/30, new lighting). (Nov 25)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.34671
