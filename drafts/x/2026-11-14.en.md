🤖 Physical AI Daily (Nov 14)
MIT's Reins harness has frontier LLMs pilot a real robot zero-shot, a cheap-rig study finds most robotics papers don't replicate, GapONet cuts the humanoid sim-to-real gap 40%+, and CoRL held its first Sim-to-Real-to-Field workshop.

---

🦾 Can frontier LLMs just drive a real robot arm?
MIT CSAIL's Reins harness has GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Astra & Claude Opus 5.5 pilot a dual-arm robot zero-shot, no task code. Sol: 88% success w/ harness vs failing without it. (CoRL 2026, Nov 12)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.16632

---

🔬 Most robotics papers don't survive a cheap rig
Shisso Robotics re-tested 36 claims from 21 papers on a $1,500 rig, not $20k+ setups. Only 12 held up; several methods lost to a trivial baseline. (Nov 13)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.low-cost-robotics-paper-retesting

---

🤖 Closing the humanoid sim-to-real gap
GapONet learns the sim-vs-real mismatch in humanoid dynamics via an operator model + RL, on a new 257K-frame dataset. Zero-shot tracking accuracy on unseen humanoids: +40% avg. (Nov 13)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.gaponet-humanoid-sim-real-gap

---

🧪 CoRL's first Sim-to-Real-to-Field workshop
Stanford, Berkeley, UW & DARPA/SRI held a CoRL 2026 workshop on moving robot policies from sim to reliable field deployment. Boston Dynamics & Berkeley gave invited talks. (Nov 12)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.sim-to-real-to-field-transfer
