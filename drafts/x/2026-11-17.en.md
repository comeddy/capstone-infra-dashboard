🕹️ An open model learns to "use" real robots like a computer
MIT/Berkeley/UCSD/NVIDIA's PEAR trains a 27B model via sim-only RL, no demos. LIBERO 1.6%→74.4%; real dual-arm robot 0%→60%. Code open. (Nov 16)
https://github.com/mit-han-lab/pear

---

🧭 A humanoid walks itself from an office lobby to the street
AgiBot's X-Nav takes a Lingxi X2 humanoid ~100m indoors-to-outdoors on one instruction, through a door, past obstacles, via RGB-only MELIOR models. (Nov 12)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.17598

---

🎥 Teaching robots from human videos never meant for them
SALA (Sharpa Robotics/SJTU/Tsinghua) links unpaired human & robot clips via language into one latent-action space. More human video = lower robot error, higher success. (Nov 12)
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.17110

---

🗂️ A new open dataset chases a "scaling law" for physical AI
Ropedia Xperience 2: 1,589 3D-scanned scenes + 4,230 egocentric recordings (131h, 74 tasks), reused for sim, motion retargeting & benchmarks. Open. (Nov 12)
https://github.com/Ropedia/Ropedia-Xperience-2
